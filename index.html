I am a B.Tech student specializing in Artificial Intelligence and Data Science, with a strong interest in building intelligent, data-driven solutions for real-world challenges. I enjoy exploring how artificial intelligence, machine learning, and data can be transformed into practical applications that create meaningful impact.

My areas of interest include Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Computer Vision, Natural Language Processing, Data Analytics, Generative AI, Geographic Information Systems (GIS), and modern web technologies. I continuously work on improving my technical knowledge by developing projects, experimenting with AI models, analyzing datasets, and learning emerging technologies.

My long-term goal is to become an AI & Data Science Engineer capable of designing and developing intelligent systems that combine artificial intelligence with real-world data and modern software engineering.

Beyond technical skills, I believe in continuous learning, creativity, collaboration, and experimentation. I am always looking for opportunities to learn new technologies, participate in challenging projects, collaborate with other developers and researchers, and turn ideas into working solutions.

My vision is simple: Learn → Build → Innovate → Create Impact.
